The Brief

Your Uniform Is the First Thing a Guest Reads

A guest forms an opinion of a property in the first ninety seconds, and almost none of that opinion comes from words. It comes from the lobby, the lighting, and the person standing behind the desk. A uniform that fits badly or has gone shiny at the elbows undoes a great deal of expensive interior design.

Mechi treats hospitality uniforms as part of the property, not as staff supplies. We work from your palette, your interiors and your service style — and we build for the reality that a front-desk shirt is worn, seen and photographed far more than most garments in a wardrobe.

The Range

What We Make for Hospitality

Front Desk & Guest Relations

Shirts, blouses, waistcoats and blazers in your property colours. Tailored silhouettes that hold their shape across a long shift under warm lobby lighting.

Food & Beverage

Service shirts, aprons, chef coats and kitchen wear. Stain-conscious fabric choices and construction built for heat, movement and repeat washing.

Housekeeping

Tunic-and-trouser sets and saree-compatible options. Built for bending, lifting and full-day wear, with fabrics that recover rather than crease.

Support & Back of House

Maintenance, security and grounds staff in coordinated but distinct uniforms — so the whole property reads as one team, department by department.

Specification

How These Are Built

Fabrics
Poly-cotton, poly-viscose, twill and blended suiting selected for drape, colour depth and behaviour under warm interior lighting.
Colour Matching
Matched to your property palette from a physical reference, not a screen. Approved swatch held on file for every reorder.
Departments
Distinct uniforms per department, unified by a shared colour and detailing language across the property.
Branding
Embroidered property logo, name badges, and department detailing in contrast piping or trim.
Quality
Piece-by-piece inspection before dispatch, with particular attention to collars, cuffs and closures — the parts guests actually see.
Turnaround
Timelines quoted upfront and committed to. Pre-season and pre-opening schedules planned backwards from your date.
