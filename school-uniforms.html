School & Education Uniforms

Where confidence begins.

Mechi makes well-fitted, durable uniforms that reflect your institution's identity — for students, faculty, sports teams and administration — without the headache of unreliable suppliers.

Why It Matters

Durability first, identity second.

A school uniform is worn every day, for years, by people who run, sit on floors, play sport and grow out of things. It has to survive the year before it can represent the institution.

Mechi builds education programmes around that reality. Fabric is chosen for daily wear and frequent washing, your crest and colours are held as a fixed specification, and every reorder matches what students and staff already wear.
