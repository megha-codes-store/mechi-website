Healthcare Uniforms

Built for the people who never stop working.

Mechi designs and manufactures complete healthcare uniform programmes for hospitals, nursing homes, clinics, diagnostic centres and medical colleges — from doctors and nursing staff to housekeeping and patient wear.

Why It Matters

A uniform your staff wear for twelve hours has to earn its place.

In a clinical environment the uniform does two jobs at once. It signals role and hierarchy instantly — patients and families need to know who is a doctor, who is nursing staff, who is support — and it has to survive industrial laundering several times a week without fading, pilling or losing shape.

Mechi builds healthcare programmes around both. Colour-coding is planned across departments so identification is immediate. Fabric is specified for wash frequency, not just for how it looks on day one. And fits are cut for movement — reaching, bending, lifting — across a full shift.
