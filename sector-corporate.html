The Brief

Consistency Is the Whole Product

A corporate uniform programme is rarely judged on a single garment. It is judged on whether the branch in Siliguri looks like the same company as the branch in Kolkata — same shade, same collar, same logo in the same place, two years apart.

That is a manufacturing and record-keeping discipline more than a design one. Mechi holds your specification — fabric, shade reference, cut, logo placement — on file, and builds every subsequent batch against it rather than against whatever is convenient that month.

The Range

What We Make for Corporate

Corporate Shirts

Formal and semi-formal shirts in cotton-rich and blended fabrics. Consistent collar and cuff construction, full size ladder, tailored female fits.

Blazers

Structured and soft-construction blazers in blended suiting. Lined, with consistent lapel and button specification across every batch.

Trousers

Formal trousers and chinos cut to match your shirt and blazer specification, in shades held against a fixed reference.

Polo T-Shirts

Mercerised and jacquard-trim polos in a wide colour range. Frontline, sales and field-team appropriate, XS to 5XL.

Jerseys & Team Wear

Event, drive and team jerseys with numbering and naming. Built for campaigns and conferences without looking disposable.

Specification

How These Are Built

Fabrics
Cotton-rich, poly-cotton, poly-viscose and blended suiting selected for drape, recovery and colour retention through office-cycle laundering.
Shade Discipline
A single approved shade reference held on file and matched batch to batch, across years and across locations.
Sizing
XS to 5XL across lines, with tailored female fits specified rather than substituted.
Branding
Left-chest embroidery standard. Sleeve, back and collar detailing, department identifiers and designation badges available.
Multi-Location
One specification serving every branch, so a nationwide rollout looks like one decision rather than several.
Reorders
New joiners outfitted against the held specification, with turnaround quoted upfront.
