Hospitality

Hospitality Uniforms

Uniforms that bring your hospitality brand to life.

Mechi designs complete uniform identities for hotels, restaurants, cafés, resorts, banquets and clubs — where every guest-facing team member is part of how your property is remembered.

Why It Matters

In hospitality, the uniform is the first thing a guest reads about you.

Before a guest speaks to anyone, they have already formed an impression from what your team is wearing. A well-cut, well-maintained uniform reads as attention to detail. A poorly fitted or faded one quietly undercuts everything else you have invested in.

Mechi approaches a hospitality programme as one identity across many roles. The front office, the restaurant floor, housekeeping and the kitchen each need different construction and different fabrics — but they should all clearly belong to the same property.

How It Works

From brief to a fully dressed team.

01

Talk to the Founders

Message or call us. Megha and Sanchit take the first call personally; our team then looks after your project, with the founders always in the loop.

02

Design & Fabric

We share design mockups and fabric swatches for your approval. Site visits and physical samples are arranged when needed.

03

Sizing

A size chart for standard sizes, trial pieces for your staff to try on, or individual measurements for each staff member.

04

Production

Manufactured under our direct quality control and checked at production. No minimum order; one to four weeks, depending on quantity.

05

Delivery

Packed department by department. Measured pieces are numbered, with a staff list; standard sizes carry size tags.

06

After Delivery

Reorders of any size, matched to your original specifications. Any fault on our side is exchanged.
