Founder Story

Megha & Sanchit

Megha and Sanchit didn't come from a garment background. They came from a place of observation. They noticed that uniform procurement in most organisations was treated as a logistics problem — bulk orders placed with whoever quoted cheapest, little thought given to fabric quality, fit, or how the wearer actually felt.

That bothered them. Because they understood that a uniform isn't just clothing. It's the first thing a person puts on before they face the world on behalf of their organisation. It shapes how they carry themselves. It either gives them confidence or quietly takes it away.

So they built Mechi. Named by combining their own names — Me from Megha, chi from Sanchit — it was personal from the very first stitch.

Carefully crafted. Confidently worn.

The philosophy Megha and Sanchit have carried from day one
Company Story

A Uniform Partner, Not a Vendor

Mechi — House of Uniforms is a premium uniform manufacturing and supply brand based in Siliguri, West Bengal, serving organisations across healthcare, hospitality, education, and corporate sectors.

Mechi operates through a process-controlled subcontracting model — selecting fabrics, setting specifications, and conducting final quality inspection across production partners in Siliguri and Kolkata. This allows Mechi to maintain consistent quality standards across thousands of pieces monthly without compromising on the care that goes into each order.

Today, Mechi serves 100+ organisational clients across West Bengal, Bihar, pan-India, and one international client in California. Mechi is not a garment shop. It is not a printing business. It is not a low-cost vendor. It is a uniform partner — one that helps organisations present their best through uniforms that are built to last, delivered on time, and backed by founders who are personally accountable for every order.
