The Brief

The Uniform Is the Institution, Worn Home Every Day

A school uniform is the most widely distributed piece of branding an institution owns. It is seen on buses, in markets, at bus stops and in family photographs. It is also worn by children who grow, fall over, and are not gentle with cloth.

Those two facts pull in opposite directions, and most suppliers pick one. Mechi holds both — a uniform that carries your institution well in public and survives the way children actually wear clothes, with a size ladder and delivery schedule that does not turn every session into an administrative emergency.

The Range

What We Make for Education

Complete School Sets

Shirts, trousers, skirts, pinafores and tunics in coordinated fabric and colour. Full size ladder from junior to senior school, cut and graded consistently.

House & Identity Wear

House t-shirts, blazers, ties, badges and sashes. Colour-held across houses and across years, so this year’s red matches last year’s.

Sports & Activity Kit

Track sets, jerseys, PT uniforms and team kit. Breathable fabrics, numbered and named on request, built for actual use rather than annual-day display.

Specification

How These Are Built

Fabrics
Poly-cotton and poly-viscose blends selected for colour fastness, shrinkage control and resilience through frequent home laundering.
Size Ladder
Graded consistently from junior to senior school, so a child moving up a size gets the same garment in a larger cut — not a different garment.
Colour Consistency
Institution colours held to a fixed reference across sessions. New admissions match returning students.
Branding
Embroidered or printed crests, house identifiers, and name tapes. Placement standardised across every piece.
Session Planning
Volumes and delivery planned against your admission calendar, with buffer stock sizing discussed upfront.
Reorders
Mid-session top-ups handled against your held specification, without re-approving fabric and colour each time.
