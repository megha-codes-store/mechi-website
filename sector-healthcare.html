Sector 01 — Healthcare

Built for Long Shifts, Lasting Comfort

Scrubs, lab coats, nurse uniforms, OT gowns and patient wear for hospitals and clinics that expect their clinical staff to look as capable as they are.

The Brief

A Twelve-Hour Garment, Not an Eight-Hour One

Healthcare uniforms fail in ways other uniforms do not. A seam that chafes becomes unbearable by hour nine. A fabric that holds heat becomes a distraction during a procedure. A colour that fades unevenly across a ward makes an entire department look tired, even when the care is excellent.

Mechi designs healthcare uniforms backwards from the shift, not forwards from the catalogue. We ask how long people are on their feet, what they carry in their pockets, how often the garment is laundered and at what temperature — then specify fabric, construction and colour against those answers.

The Range

What We Make for Healthcare

Scrubs

Two-piece sets in breathable poly-cotton and poly-viscose blends. Multiple pocket configurations, drawstring or elasticated waist, unisex and tailored female fits.

Lab Coats

Full-sleeve and half-sleeve, knee-length and mid-thigh. Reinforced button placket, three-pocket standard, optional side vents and back belt.

Nurse Uniforms

Tunic-and-trouser and dress formats. Designed for ease of movement, modest coverage and consistent silhouette across a full nursing team.

OT Gowns

Surgical and procedure gowns with wrap-around closure, tie-back fastening and fabric weights selected for autoclave and high-temperature laundry cycles.

Patient Wear

Gowns, pyjama sets and delivery wear. Easy-access closures, soft-handle fabrics, and colour coding by ward or department on request.

Waterproof Aprons

Fluid-resistant aprons for cleaning, laboratory and support roles. Wipe-clean surface, reinforced neck and waist ties.
