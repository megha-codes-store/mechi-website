Mechi — House of Uniforms is a premium uniform manufacturing and supply partner based in Siliguri, West Bengal, serving healthcare, hospitality, education, and corporate organisations across Siliguri, Kolkata, Bihar, and pan-India, with international reach including a client in California, USA. Mechi specialises in scrubs, lab coats, nurse uniforms, OT gowns, patient wear, corporate shirts, blazers, trousers, polo t-shirts, jerseys, and complete uniform solutions with custom logo embroidery and printing. Founder-led and process-controlled, Mechi delivers premium bulk and wholesale uniform orders with consistent quality across every batch.
