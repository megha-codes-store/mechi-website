How It Works

From brief to a fully dressed team.

01

Talk to the Founders

Message or call us. Megha and Sanchit take every project personally, from the first conversation.

02

Design & Fabric

We share design mockups and fabric swatches for your approval. Site visits and physical samples are arranged when needed.

03

Sizing

A size chart for standard sizes, or individual measurements taken for your staff.

04

Production

Manufactured under our direct quality control and checked at production. No minimum order; one to four weeks, depending on quantity.

05

Delivery

Packed department by department. Measured pieces are numbered, with a staff list; standard sizes carry size tags.

06

After Delivery

Reorders of any size, matched to your original specifications. Any fault on our side is exchanged.

Mechi — House of Uniforms is a premium uniform manufacturing and supply partner based in Siliguri, West Bengal, serving healthcare, hospitality, education, and corporate organisations across Siliguri, Kolkata, Bihar, and pan-India, with international reach including a client in California, USA. Mechi specialises in scrubs, lab coats, nurse uniforms, OT gowns, patient wear, corporate shirts, blazers, trousers, polo t-shirts, jerseys, and complete uniform solutions with custom logo embroidery and printing. Founder-led and process-controlled, Mechi delivers premium bulk and wholesale uniform orders with consistent quality across every batch.
